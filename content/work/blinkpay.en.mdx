## What it is

Blinkpay is a small experiment: use Solana Blinks (single-action payment links) as the
merchant-side rail for USDC settlement, targeting the layer of Brazilian small businesses
that currently runs on Pix.

The pitch is not that Blinks replace Pix — Pix is excellent, free, and already ubiquitous.
The pitch is that for a specific merchant, at a specific price point, receiving USDC
directly instead of BRL-then-conversion removes one round trip through the banking system
and one FX surprise.

## Design

A merchant sets up a payee address once. Each sale generates a signed Blink URL encoded
into a QR code; the buyer taps it, their wallet auto-completes the transfer, USDC lands
in the merchant address in under a second. A small backend records the sale for
reconciliation and generates a Brazilian invoice.

## Where it is
